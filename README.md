# Analysing Sales Performance 

### Business Challenges 
- Total sales across different store locations and time periods
- Comparing top performing & underperforming departments against regional targets
- Determine if inventory adjustments are needed
- Approach to assist marketing team optimising their promotional calendar

### Dataset
Dataset was included in the course material from CFI. 

### Tools
Power BI and Power Query for Data Modelling, Transformation, Analysis and Visualisation

### Key Takeaways 
- Texas stores demonstrated the highest sales contribution, with clothing emerging as the top-performing department
- Significant peak observed towards the end of each year, indicating strong seasonal demand
- Overall sales performance lagged behind target by approx. 34%
- Inventory optimisation and marketing alignment needed, such as promotions to align with seasonal demand patterns

### Recommendations
- Determine factors driving strong sales performances in Texas
- Refine demand forecasting models to anticipate seasonal peak demands in different departments
- Understand sales shortfall in New Mexico, South Carolina, etc.
